Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Who we are

Teo's Seizures operates a private, non-commercial text message (SMS) tool used by one family to keep a log of a family member's seizures and medication times. It is not a public service and is not open to sign-up by the public.

Information we collect

  • The mobile phone numbers of the small group of family caregivers (about 2 to 4 people) who have agreed to use the tool.
  • The text messages those caregivers send to the tool, such as dates, times, durations, medication names and doses, and short notes.
  • Screenshots of stopwatch timers that caregivers choose to send, which are read only to find a time and a duration.
  • The replies the tool sends back.

How we use it

We use this information only to keep the log, to send confirmations, follow-up questions, answers to caregivers' questions, safety alerts, and a daily summary email to the family members who have agreed to receive it.

Service providers

To run the tool we use trusted service providers: Twilio (to send and receive text messages), Anthropic (an AI service that reads incoming messages and screenshots so they can be recorded), and an email provider (to deliver the daily summary). These providers handle information only to provide their service to us. The log itself is stored as a spreadsheet kept by the account owner.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not share it with anyone other than the service providers above and the family members who use the tool.

Keeping and deleting information

Information is kept for as long as the family uses the tool. Any caregiver can ask us to remove their phone number and messages at any time by contacting us at the email address below.

Your choices

You can stop receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help.
