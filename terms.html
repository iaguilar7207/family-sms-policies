About this service

Teo's Seizures operates a private text message (SMS) tool used by one family to log a family member's seizures and medication times. Only family caregivers who have been invited and have agreed to take part may use it.

SMS Terms

  • Program: Caregivers receive confirmations of logged entries, follow-up questions, answers to questions about the log, and safety alerts.
  • Consent: A caregiver is enrolled only after agreeing to receive messages from the account owner. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Nothing is sold.
  • Message frequency: Message frequency varies depending on how much is logged.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages.
  • Help: Reply HELP for help, or contact us at 2025aguilars@gmail.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Not medical advice or an emergency service

This tool only records what caregivers text to it and repeats it back. It does not give medical advice and does not replace a care plan, a doctor, or a pharmacist. Alerts or replies may be delayed or may fail to arrive, so do not rely on this tool for emergencies. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.
